Ring size guide

Measure before you order

Confirm your size before ordering or bespoke manufacture.

  1. Wrap a thin strip of paper around the finger.
  2. Mark where it overlaps, then measure in millimetres.
  3. Match the circumference to a UK/SA ring size.
  4. For wide bands, go half a size up.
