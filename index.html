Ring size guide

Measure before you order

For fine rings, customers should confirm size before ordering or bespoke manufacture. This launch guide gives a simple measuring method until a printable sizer is added.

  1. Wrap a thin strip of paper around the finger.
  2. Mark where the paper overlaps, then measure in millimetres.
  3. Match the circumference to a UK/SA ring size before purchase.
  4. For wide bands, consider going half a size up.
